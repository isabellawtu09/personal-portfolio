## Overview
 
Full tilt systems are known for controlling large populations of robots, our Burnout Model has a unique limitation where it has only a certain amount of moves, afterwards it turns into an obstacle for other robots. Using these robots we use their limitations to create different forms of computation. By strategically using burnt-out bots to form pathways, we design and validate gadgets for universal logic gates NAND and Fan-out, proving that the model is capable of universal computation.
